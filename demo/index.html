Why this is the whole thing

Never wrong out loud. It answers only from vetted, cited facts, labels what is live versus roadmap, and refuses when it doesn't know. The refusal is the product, not a bug.

Where a wrong answer is expensive. Banks, insurers, health systems, government. One misstated security posture or "yes we do that" about a roadmap feature isn't a lost deal. It's a compliance event.

The refusal you just saw is the moat. Anyone can bolt a copilot onto a call. Almost no one builds it so being correct is the point, with a test suite proving it never confidently lies.

This is a working engine. The grounding logic runs right here in your browser over sample data. No answer is generated. What you read is the vetted record, or nothing.

And it holds through a bad transcript. On the live call, when the buyer names a core the transcriber mishears, it resolves on the part it got right and answers correctly instead of confusing a lookalike for a yes. A wrong transcript costs a miss, never a confident wrong answer. That trade is the whole design.
